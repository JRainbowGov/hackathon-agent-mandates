# Question 10: "It's doing the absence letters. Just give it what it needs."

**Open question.** No eval cases and no required design.

**The problem.** A mandate's scope is JSON, written by hand on the command line:
`[{"server": "pupil-data", "tools": ["search"], "constraints": {"urn": ["SCH01"]}}]`. The administrator cannot write
that and cannot read it. Someone, or something, turns their sentence into a scope, and they sign the result without
knowing what it says.

**Worth working out:**

- Who turns the sentence into a scope: a form, a template for each kind of job, or a model?
- How does the person check what they are about to sign, in words they understand? Does that description match the
  scope exactly, or only roughly?
- "What it needs" is the agent's opinion. If a model writes the scope, what stops the request being talked wider?
- What happens to a request that is too vague to scope?

**What to show:** the administrator's sentence, the mandate written from it and what they were shown before signing, a
request it allows, a request it denies, and one sentence on what is still unresolved.
